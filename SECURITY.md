# Security policy

Please do not post a suspected Nappio vulnerability, recovery credential, pairing code, TURN credential, or private monitoring detail in a public issue.

Report security concerns privately to `tmoreton89@gmail.com` with the subject **Nappio security**. Include the affected app/Worker version, a concise reproduction, and the impact. Remove secrets, live room credentials, and identifiable audio/video from screenshots or logs.

Nappio is maintained as a small free application, so response times are best effort. Reports that could expose a live monitoring room or credentials take priority.
